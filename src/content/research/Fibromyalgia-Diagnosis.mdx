<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

Developed a machine learning approach to fibromyalgia diagnosis using HRV, GSR, and EMG signals, achieving approximately 94% test accuracy.

<h2 id="details">Details</h2>

Combined feature scaling, GAN augmentation, and GCN embeddings with a 2D CNN. Placed 3rd among 60+ Computer Science projects at the Delaware Valley Science Fairs and 1st at the CCRSC fair (2025).
