<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

As founder and researcher at Socci Group, I engineered Ridge, Log-Ridge, and KNN regression models to estimate U.S. post-hurricane economic damage using atmospheric and geolocation data. The models were trained on 17 storms and tested on 10.

<h2 id="engineering-and-lessons">Engineering &amp; Lessons</h2>

With limited historical storm data, I used feature reduction and regularization to address severe overfitting. The project highlighted the importance of transparent baseline models as calibration tools when working with small datasets.
