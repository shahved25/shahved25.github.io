<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

As founder and head coach of Ace Tennis Academy LLC, I operated a youth tennis academy over four seasons, coached 120+ players, and hired eight coaches. I managed operations, financial planning, marketing, web design, and sales.

<h2 id="engineering-and-lessons">Engineering &amp; Lessons</h2>

Court double-bookings, severe weather disruptions, and initial hiring challenges led me to put stronger operational protocols in place. The experience taught me to pair core business operations with adaptability as real-world conditions change.
